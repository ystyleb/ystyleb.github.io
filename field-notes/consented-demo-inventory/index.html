Work through the checklist before screen sharing

  • Review an exported thumbnail and its surrounding browser tabs. Keep original customer photos in a private folder.
  • Use audio or voice material only with clear permission for public playback. Keep a transcript or licensed sample as fallback.
  • Show a synthetic playtest result if the real session contains private handles or puzzle spoilers.

The public view outputs only the ID of an approved asset. This worksheet doesn’t change permissions or verify rights and nothing is uploaded.
