Compare timed and untimed puzzle completion

When a browser puzzle uses a timer, check whether people understand the prompt before they run out of time. Compare fresh groups on an equivalent task. Report the number of people who completed the task rather than treating timeout as a wrong answer.

Ask after each attempt

  1. What did you think the prompt wanted?
  2. When did you know what to do next?
  3. Did the timer change which answer you were prepared to try?

Use synthetic prompts if the real round should remain secret. The worksheet reports descriptive rates and does not establish a statistically reliable effect.
