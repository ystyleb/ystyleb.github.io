An image wipe for aligned exports

Slide between two exports to catch differences around a seam, silhouette or label. Choose files with identical dimensions whose subjects were aligned before export. The comparison runs locally in this tab; it does not upload or store either file.

Choose two still PNG, WebP or JPEG files, or try the sample below.

Start with the example

The sample uses two fictional navy jackets. Put the divider near the cuff to see a shifted trim line, then move it through the pockets. The button and pocket are where they were in the first drawing. Contrast this with an unaligned pair, where many edges shift together.

Interpret the change

Pick one fixed landmark and check both sides there first. If an entire figure shifts, align the images in your editor before comparing details. A small display canvas can hide a few pixels of change. Open both originals at full size if the details matter.
