Measure the empty border in a transparent image

A PNG can be 512 pixels wide while the actual artwork uses only half that space. Drop in a transparent asset and measure how much of the canvas survives at the opacity threshold you choose. The sample has an off-centre shape with a faint shadow.

Compare 1 and 128. If the margins change, a soft shadow or partially transparent outline is controlling the footprint. An opaque photo fills its canvas and will show zero margins. Measurements use the original pixel grid. Files stay in your browser.

Made by Winson. I use this check before resizing a chat graphic in Emote Resizer or aligning assets in Free Image Merger. For a layout that mixes cutouts with preview images from my other projects, TryOnSwap and PawMotif, measure the exported asset you actually plan to place. A rectangular background counts as visible.
