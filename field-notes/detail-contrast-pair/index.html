Compare two colors before shrinking a graphic

Pick a foreground detail and the area immediately behind it. The swatches show how the pair looks at two sizes. The ratio measures relative luminance; a textured edge can still disappear when you reduce the image.

Use the result

  1. Sample the interior of the feature and the neighboring background, avoiding half-transparent edge pixels. Try a second neighboring patch for a striped or mottled background.
  2. Compare the two previews at normal zoom. If a detail disappears at the small size, test a different lightness or a wider outline before changing the entire palette.
  3. For a numerical check, black against white gives 21.00 to 1. Matching swatches give 1.00 to 1. The calculation uses linearized sRGB and the lighter and darker luminances.
