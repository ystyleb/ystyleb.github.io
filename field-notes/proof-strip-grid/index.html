How many proof cards fit on one sheet?

Lay out a sheet of thumbnail or print proofs before exporting a large file. The calculator tries both orientations, reserves a gutter around each cut and reports unused space. Measurements are in millimetres.

Try it on paper

Start with a 210 × 297 mm sheet, an 8 mm border, 65 × 87 mm cards and 3 mm gaps. Two upright columns fit. Turn the cards sideways and four rows of two fit. Draw the grid before choosing which crop to enlarge.

The result assumes all cards share an orientation and that the printer can reach the chosen margin. Print one sheet at actual size. Measure a card edge with a ruler before cutting the rest.

Where to try it

  • Emote Resizer — Use the original artwork for a thumbnail proof; check the exported emotes separately at their actual pixel sizes.
  • PawMotif — Compare a face crop and a full portrait at the dimensions of the intended card.
  • TryOnSwap — Place outfit options at equal scale so a different crop cannot disguise a change in garment length.
