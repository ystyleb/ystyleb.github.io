Try the default example on paper

A 1200 × 800 photo fills a 600 × 600 square at 75% scale. The result is 900 pixels wide, so the frame hides 150 output pixels on each side. In the original photo that is 200 source pixels per side. Fitting the entire image instead produces a 600 × 400 picture, leaving 100 pixels of blank space above and below.

Four decisions before exporting

  • For a chat graphic in Emote Resizer, compare a square with a tight source crop. Leave room around the expression before checking at chat size.
  • For a two-photo comparison in Free Image Merger, calculate the panel size, rather than entering the size of the entire collage. Use the same panel shape for both images.
  • When presenting a generated outfit from TryOnSwap, check whether the retained rectangle includes the cuff and hem you want to discuss.
  • For a portrait proof from PawMotif, see whether a square crop would touch an ear or a collar tag. A border can preserve details near the original edge.
