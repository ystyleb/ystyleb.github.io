Measure whether two people score puzzle answers consistently

When a prompt accepts open answers, ask two reviewers to judge a small set independently. Keep their first decisions intact, then discuss the cases where they disagreed.

Interpret the disagreement

  1. Review disagreement notes by type: spelling variant, overly broad category, or disputed wording in the prompt.
  2. Add at most one clarification per repeated type. Ask a new reviewer to apply the revised rule to a second sample.
  3. Record the four counts, especially when nearly all answers are accepted. High straight agreement can hide a rare but important scoring issue.

The starting counts are fictional. The worksheet reports raw agreement and does not measure chance-corrected agreement or game quality.
