Which part of a photo survives a centered cover crop?

A square preview can hide the ears, cuffs or fine marks at the edge of a wide image. Enter the source and output dimensions to see the actual source rectangle retained by a centered cover crop.

  2. Mark the outermost detail you need to retain in the original. If it lies outside that rectangle, change the composition, add padding, or change the target proportions.
  3. Browsers and image editors may round a fractional crop edge to a device pixel. This worksheet assumes a centered cover fit and an image that has already been rotated upright.
