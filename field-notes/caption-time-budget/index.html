Plan a readable caption window before exporting a clip

Fit a caption to the time the words remain on screen. Enter your own reading-rate budget and turn a combined block into several cues when the display is crowded.

Check it in context

  1. Use cue timings from the actual edit. Start markers on silences only when that still matches the speaker or singer.
  2. Keep names and short, meaningful phrases together. Break a cue at a natural boundary and check that line wraps work on a narrow display.
  3. Watch it once muted on a small screen. Ask someone to tell you what mattered without pausing the clip.

This planning estimate counts Unicode code points after collapsing whitespace. A composite emoji may count as several; your own speed target and an actual playback check matter more than the estimate.
