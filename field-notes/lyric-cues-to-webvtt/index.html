Turn timed lyric lines into a WebVTT caption file

Play your clip and note the start of each lyric in seconds. Enter one cue per line as seconds | words. Set the last cue’s end to the clip duration. The converter ends each cue when the next line starts.

Preview the file with your actual recording. Include spoken words even when the beat begins earlier, and split long lines at a natural breath. A short instrumental gap in the middle needs an earlier end time edited in the downloaded file.
Made by Winson. I built this for people captioning a gift-song excerpt from Songifted or a short rap clip from MakeItRap. The sample lines above are original.
