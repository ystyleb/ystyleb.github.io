The surprise gift handoff sheet

Could someone else finish your gift if you had to hand it over tomorrow? Pick one route, fill in the three lines, then ask a friend to read it back. Print the page if you want to keep it. The boxes stay in this page and clear when you reload.

Three lines to hand over

Choose a route and give the maker one check

A sung memory. Speak the sentence aloud. Circle the word that should land on the long note. Give the maker the pronunciation of any name. A sample handoff for Songifted would read “Put the long note on home, and say Mei like May.”

A rap dedication. Turn two details into a call and an answer. Clap four beats per line and remove a word wherever your breath runs out. For a MakeItRap brief, underline the last word you want to hear in the hook.

A pet portrait. Label left and right from the pet’s point of view. Give the maker one well-lit reference and list two markings that distinguish this pet. When reviewing a draft from PawMotif, cover the background and look for those two marks first.

A clothing mood card. Use a garment photo you may share and a consenting subject. Agree on one visual detail, such as the collar or sleeve shape. If you use TryOnSwap to illustrate a look, mark the image “style preview” and check actual measurements before ordering a garment.

The read-back

Hand the sheet to a friend without explaining it. Ask “What would you keep?” and “What would you leave out?” If they guess, replace one adjective with a detail they could point to.
