Level-match two audio clips for a listening comparison

A louder export can seem more exciting even when the arrangement is the same. Measure the same section of two recordings in your audio editor, then calculate a gain offset for each. Enter average RMS levels in dBFS and sample peaks in dBFS.

Try it

Use the result

  1. Use equal-duration regions with comparable vocals. Exclude leading silence and measure both in the same editor using the same RMS setting.
  2. Apply the suggested offsets to listening copies. Both clips keep at least 1 dB of sample-peak headroom. The quieter average is the reference, so this tool never boosts a clip.
  3. Switch between the two versions at a fixed playback volume. RMS approximates average energy. Different spectra can still sound unequally loud, and a sample peak cannot predict every encoded true peak.
