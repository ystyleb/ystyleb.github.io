Make a pronunciation cue card for a vocalist

Prepare one plain-text note for an approved name or phrase. The person with the name should confirm the cue before recording. Use the starter example as a placeholder and substitute a consented sample offline.

Use the cue in a recording session

  1. Ask the speaker to say it in a plain sentence. Write the rhythm they actually use and confirm it with them.
  2. Keep the approved spelling separate from the artist cue. Captions and notes should show the approved spelling.
  3. Test the phrase both on its own and beside the previous words. Leave enough space for the name to remain recognizable.

This page does not supply a pronunciation or send anything to a server.
