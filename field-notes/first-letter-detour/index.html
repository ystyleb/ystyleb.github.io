The first-letter detour

A solo warm-up for category games. Name the first answer that comes to mind. Cross out its initial, then find a second answer in the same category that starts with a different letter.

Something that tells time

If “clock” comes first, take C off the table. Where else might you look for the time?

See one possible detour

Sundial. Thinking about a shadow instead of a clock face gets you to S.

Make your own card

Pick a category that fits both answers. “Things on a desk” may exclude a sundial, while “something that tells time” includes both a sundial and a clock. Write your starting answer on one side of a card and the different-initial answer on the back. If both depend on the same mental picture, try changing the place or the material.
